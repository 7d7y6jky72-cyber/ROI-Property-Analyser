How returns are calculated

Return on current equity = (annual net cash flow + annualised capital growth) ÷ current equity. This highlights how effectively today's tied-up equity is working.
Capital growth is annualised from purchase price/date to current valuation. Net cash flow includes rent less operating expenses and interest, but excludes principal repayments so financing structure can be compared consistently. This version is an analysis tool, not tax or financial advice.
